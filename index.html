Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes your log.

How is the daily norm calculated? With the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, adjusted for your activity and goal, and never below 1200 kcal (women) or 1500 kcal (men). It is an estimate, not medical advice.

Where does product data come from? From Open Food Facts — data © Open Food Facts contributors, under the Open Database License. Found a wrong value? You can fix it on Open Food Facts.
